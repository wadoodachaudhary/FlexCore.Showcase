<h1>Grid — Assembly Line Items</h1>
<p>
    A heavier grid modeled after HomeFront's <code>FAssembly</code> form: editable
    line items with vendor, PO index, description, qty, UOM, unit price, total —
    plus a sum footer over <strong>Total</strong>, grouping by PO index, drag-reorder
    headers, and per-row formatting.
</p>

<div class="demo-frame">
    <GridControl TValue="AssemblyItem"
                 DataSource="@_items"
                 AllowSelection="true"
                 AllowSorting="true"
                 AllowFiltering="true"
                 AllowGrouping="true"
                 AllowResizing="true"
                 AllowColumnReorder="true"
                 EnableHover="true"
                 ShowSearchBar="true"
                 Height="520px"
                 AggregateRows="_aggregates">
        <GridColumnsBase>
            <GridColumn Field="@nameof(AssemblyItem.PriceLevel)"  HeaderText="Price Level" Width="160px" />

            <GridColumn Field="@nameof(AssemblyItem.POIndex)"     HeaderText="PO Index"    Width="120px" />
            <GridColumn Field="@nameof(AssemblyItem.Description)" HeaderText="Description" Width="240px" />
            <GridColumn Field="@nameof(AssemblyItem.Qty)"         HeaderText="Qty"         Width="80px"

<h2>Things to try</h2>
<ul>
    <li>Drag the <strong>PO Index</strong> column header onto the group panel — sums update per group.</li>
    <li>Click any header to sort ascending / descending / clear.</li>
    <li>Click the filter icon on a header to filter; combine with sort.</li>
    <li>Drag column headers left/right to reorder; widths persist.</li>
    <li>Search across all columns using the search bar above the headers.</li>
</ul>
